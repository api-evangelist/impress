## What Impress sells

Impress sells orthodontic treatment, not software. Its products are clear aligner
treatment for adults, teens and children, clear retainers (sold as an annual
subscription), and professional teeth whitening, delivered through owned clinics and
booked from the consumer website. It is an official Invisalign provider in the US.

`api.smile2impress.com` is a live host and resolves to AWS, but it is the private
backend for the Impress patient app and the Impress Clinics practitioner app. Every
anonymous request to it — root, spec paths, `.well-known` paths — returns
`403 {"message":"Forbidden"}`. It is not a developer surface.
